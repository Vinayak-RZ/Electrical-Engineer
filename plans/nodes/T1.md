1. Happy `solve-circuit-problem` (checked or exact `unchecked`)

5. Injection fixture does not flip policy
6. Photo confirm, **no** sim
7. compose-from-parts ask / fail-closed
8. Control Bode/step artifacts
9. One other-pack solve or cannot-do demonstrated

Record pass/fail and screenshots/log paths. Failures become tests or cannot-do rows — not silent.
